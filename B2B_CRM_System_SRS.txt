1. Introduction

The B2B CRM System is a web-based enterprise customer relationship management platform designed to centralize lead, contact, company, deal, activity, task, automation, and sales analytics information. The system provides sales teams and managers with a single source of truth for managing customer relationships and the complete sales lifecycle.

The system is intended for business-to-business organizations that need to replace scattered spreadsheets, email-based follow-ups, chat messages, and disconnected records with a unified CRM workflow. The platform emphasizes visibility, collaboration, automation, and data-driven sales decisions.





2. Problem Statement

B2B sales teams frequently manage customer information across spreadsheets, emails, messaging platforms, personal notes, and separate reporting tools. This fragmentation makes it difficult to maintain accurate customer histories, track leads, coordinate team activities, follow up on opportunities, and forecast revenue.





Leads and opportunities can be lost or overlooked.





Customer information is distributed across multiple tools.





Sales representatives may lack complete customer context.





Follow-ups and tasks can be missed.





Managers have limited real-time visibility into the sales pipeline.





Sales forecasting and performance reporting can require manual work.





Teams may duplicate work because customer ownership and activity are unclear.

The proposed CRM addresses these issues by providing one centralized platform for customer data, sales processes, team activities, automation, and analytics.





3. Scope

The project includes the following major areas:





User authentication and role-based access control.





Lead management and lead qualification.





Contact and company/account management.





Customer 360° profiles with relationship history.





Deal and sales pipeline management.





Task, reminder, and activity management.





Workflow automation and follow-up support.





Team collaboration and ownership of records.





Sales analytics, dashboards, reports, and forecasting.





Search, filtering, sorting, and record organization.





Notifications for important activities and deadlines.

Out of scope for the initial release:





Full accounting and financial management.





Payroll and human resource management.





Advanced marketing campaign execution.





Native mobile applications.





Complex third-party integrations beyond those specifically approved for the project.





4. Objectives





Centralize B2B customer and sales information in one platform.





Provide a clear and manageable sales pipeline from lead capture to closed deal.





Improve visibility into customer relationships and communication history.





Reduce missed follow-ups through tasks, reminders, and automation.





Improve collaboration between sales representatives and managers.





Provide real-time KPIs and analytics for informed decision-making.





Support sales forecasting and performance monitoring.





Reduce manual data handling and duplicated work.





Provide a scalable, secure, and user-friendly enterprise web application.

FR-11

Deal Information

FR-12

Tasks & Reminders

Users shall be able to create, assign, prioritize, complete, and track sales tasks.

FR-13

Activities

The system shall record meetings, calls, emails, notes, and other customer-related activities.

FR-14

Workflow Automation

Authorized users shall be able to configure approved automated actions such as reminders and follow-up tasks.

FR-15

Notifications

The system shall notify users about relevant deadlines, assignments, and important activities.

FR-16

Search & Filters

Users shall be able to search and filter leads, contacts, companies, and deals.

FR-17

Dashboard

The system shall provide KPIs such as total leads, active deals, pipeline value, conversion rate, and revenue forecast.

FR-18

Analytics

The system shall provide revenue trends, conversion funnels, pipeline forecasts, lead-source performance, and sales performance.

FR-19

Reports

Authorized users shall be able to generate and view sales and performance reports.

FR-20

Data Export

Authorized users shall be able to export permitted business data and reports in supported formats.

FR-21

Audit Trail

FR-22

9. System Constraints





The system requires a supported web browser and network connectivity.





Access to data and features depends on the assigned user role and permissions.





The initial release is focused on web-based CRM functionality.





The quality of analytics depends on the completeness and accuracy of entered business data.





Third-party integrations, if added, are constrained by the APIs, authentication methods, quotas, and availability of those services.





System performance depends on hosting resources, database configuration, network conditions, and data volume.





The project schedule, budget, and available development resources may limit advanced functionality in the first release.





Business rules such as pipeline stages, permissions, and required fields must be agreed with the client or project supervisor.





10. Assumptions





Users have valid accounts and appropriate permissions before accessing protected features.





The organization will provide accurate lead, customer, company, and deal information.





Users have basic computer and web application skills.





The organization has reliable internet access for normal system operation.





Management will define the sales pipeline stages and business KPIs required by the organization.





The client will provide or approve branding, business terminology, and data policies.





A suitable database and hosting environment will be available for deployment.





System backups and access policies will be configured according to organizational requirements.

11.1 Primary Sales Lifecycle

The core business flow of the CRM is designed around the following lifecycle:





Capture a lead from an approved source.





Qualify the lead based on business criteria.





Convert or associate the lead with a contact/company.





Create and manage a deal opportunity.





Move the deal through the sales pipeline.





Schedule and complete activities and follow-ups.





Close the deal as Won or Lost.





Use analytics and reports to evaluate performance and improve future sales decisions.
